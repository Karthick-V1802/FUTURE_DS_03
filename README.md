Excited to share my Marketing Funnel Analysis Dashboard project!

As part of my internship at Future Interns, I developed a Marketing Funnel Dashboard to analyze the customer journey, evaluate marketing channel performance, and identify opportunities to improve conversion rates.

The dashboard provides a visual overview of the marketing funnel, conversion rates by channel, drop-offs at each stage, and profit distribution. I also corrected the referral channel conversion calculations and ensured that the profit distribution totals add up to 100%, improving the accuracy and reliability of the analysis.

📈 Key Features:
* Marketing Funnel Overview – Visitors, Leads, Qualified Leads, Opportunities, and Customers
* Conversion Analysis – Compared performance across Organic, Paid Search, Social, Email, and Referral channels
* Drop-off Analysis – Identified stages where potential customers leave the funnel
* Profit Distribution – Visualized the contribution of different categories to total profit
* Data Validation – Corrected referral channel calculations and verified profit distribution totals

💻 Tools & Skills: HTML • Data Analysis • Data Visualization • Marketing Analytics • Conversion Rate Analysis • Dashboard Development

Working on this project helped me improve my analytical thinking, understand customer conversion patterns, and learn how data-driven insights can help businesses optimize their marketing strategies.
